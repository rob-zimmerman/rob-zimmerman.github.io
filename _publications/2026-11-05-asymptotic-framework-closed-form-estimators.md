<b>Abstract:</b> We present a general framework for replacing estimators defined implicitly by estimating equations with explicit approximations while controlling their accuracy. The theory covers parameters approaching the boundary of the parameter space or diverging, and gives conditions under which an approximate estimator has the same first-order asymptotic distribution as its exact counterpart. Applications to gamma, zeta, von Mises, generalized Pareto, and gamma-regression models illustrate scalar and vector parameters, independent and non-identically distributed settings, and both normal and nonnormal limiting regimes. The examples are supported by simulation studies, with a real-data application for gamma regression.

<b>Keywords:</b> Estimating equations, maximum likelihood, asymptotic theory, closed-form estimators, surrogate equations.
